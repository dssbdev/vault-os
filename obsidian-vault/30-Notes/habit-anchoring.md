# Habit Anchoring

## Overview

New routines survive when they're bolted to something that already happens every time - a trip, a meal, a commute. Routines that need their own dedicated slot and their own act of willpower die in the first busy week.

## Key concepts

**Anchor, then action.** "After the Sunday shop, the weekly review." The anchor does the remembering; you only do the action.

**Pick anchors that can't be skipped.** The shop happens because there's no food otherwise. The commute happens because work does. A gym class is not an anchor - it's another habit.

**Small enough to survive a bad day.** If the action can't be done on the worst evening of the week, it will be skipped on that evening, and skipping once is how it ends.

## Practical application

Every routine that has survived a year is anchored: meal planning to the shop ([[meal-planning-basics]]), the review to the meal planning ([[weekly-review-method]]), the bike check to the Sunday ride ([[bike-maintenance-basics]]), commuting to fixed workdays ([[bike-commute]]).
