# Portuguese Pronunciation

## Overview

European Portuguese swallows its unstressed vowels, which is why it sounds like Russian to newcomers and why reading it aloud is a terrible guide to hearing it.

## Key concepts

**Nasal vowels.** *Pão* (bread), *mãe* (mother), *não* (no). The tilde means air through the nose. Getting *pão* wrong is how every bakery conversation starts badly.

**Unstressed *e* nearly vanishes.** *Telefone* sounds closer to "tlfon". Listening, not reading, is the only fix.

**S at the end sounds like "sh".** *Dois pastéis* - "doish pashtéish". Once heard, it's everywhere.

**LH and NH.** *Trabalho*, *vinho* - like the "lli" in million and the "ny" in canyon.

## Practical application

Ten minutes of radio while cooking, repeating anything that sounds odd. Words that keep failing go on the [[spaced-repetition]] list.
