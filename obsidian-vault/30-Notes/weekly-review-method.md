# The Weekly Review

## Overview

Fifteen minutes at the boundary between two weeks, asking three questions: what moved, what got carried, and what pattern is that. The first two are bookkeeping. The third is the only part nothing else in the system produces.

## Key concepts

**It's a transition, not a task.** A review only works on the quiet side of the week boundary - Sunday evening, not Monday morning, when the new week is already making demands. Worked out in [[2026-08-05-review-cadence]].

**Attach it to something unavoidable.** Mine follows the Sunday shop. See [[habit-anchoring]].

**Never restart a streak.** A missed week means the next review covers two. Restarting from zero is what killed every previous attempt.

**Name the pattern, then change the plan.** A pattern that doesn't change next week's plan was just a diary entry. [[2026-08-16-weekly-review]] is the first review where naming one ("hard tasks after easy ones don't happen") changed the plan: the hard thing is now the main task in [[today]], and it goes first.

**End with the board check.** Anything that got opened outside the cap in [[roadmap]] goes back to the inbox. Every second Sunday the review also closes the sprint and opens the next one.

## Practical application

Open the week's daily plans side by side. Count the carried tasks. Any task carried five times is not a task - it's an undecided decision, so decide it. The review itself is a log, `90-Logs/YYYY-MM/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md`.
