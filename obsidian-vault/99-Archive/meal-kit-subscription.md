# Meal-kit subscription

> **Archived 2026-05-25. Superseded by [[meal-planning-basics]].** Kept for the reasoning, not the plan.

## The plan, at the time

Pay for a weekly box of pre-portioned ingredients and recipe cards, so that deciding what to eat stops being a nightly problem. Three meals a week, cancel anytime.

## What happened

It solved the deciding, which was the real problem — and that turned out to be all it solved. The boxes cost several times the ingredients, the recipes were new every week (novelty is expensive, as [[meal-planning-basics]] later put it), and the packaging filled the recycling bin by Wednesday.

## Why archived, not deleted

Because it proved what the actual problem was. The replacement does the deciding on Saturday for free. Cancelled in the [[2026-05-25-subscription-audit]].
