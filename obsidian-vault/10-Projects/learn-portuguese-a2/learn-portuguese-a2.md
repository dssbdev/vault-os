# Portuguese A2

Two years of "I'll pick it up" produced the ability to order coffee and apologise for not speaking Portuguese. This project replaces vague exposure with a course, a weekly tutor slot, and an exam date - because a date is the only thing that has ever made me study.

## Goal

Pass the A2 exam in November. The real goal sits behind it: a ten-minute conversation with the neighbours that doesn't collapse into English.

## Scope

In: the [[portuguese-a2-course]], one tutor session a week, ten minutes of listening a day, the mock exam. Out: grammar books read cover to cover, a second app, Brazilian-Portuguese material (useful, but a different accent to train on).

## Phrases that keep coming up

> *Desculpe, pode repetir mais devagar?* - Sorry, can you say that again more slowly?
> *Ainda estou a aprender.* - I'm still learning.
> *Fica para a próxima.* - Next time, then.

The second one is the most useful sentence in the language. The pronunciation notes are in [[portuguese-pronunciation]].
