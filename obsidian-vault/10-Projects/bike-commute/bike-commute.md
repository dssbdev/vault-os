# Bike commute

**Board:** Done, reference only ([[roadmap]]).

Replace the bus with the bike three days a week. The project was the setup - route, lights, a lock that isn't decorative, somewhere to change - and it finished when the commute stopped needing a decision every morning.

## Goal

Ride three days a week for four consecutive weeks without planning it the night before. Met in June.

## What made it work

The trial in [[2026-05-31-bike-commute-trial]] is mostly a record of what *didn't*: the faster main-road route, which was miserable, and the "I'll ride when it's sunny" rule, which meant never. What worked was fixed days - Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, matching the reliable evenings in [[roadmap#How the system runs|the shape of the week]] - and a bag that stays packed.
