# Balcony herbs

Grow the three herbs I buy every week and throw half of. Small, cheap, and pleasant - which is exactly why it got deferred: it competes for the same evenings as [[recipe-box-app]] and wins on fun while losing on value.

## Why deferred, not dropped

Spring is the right time to start anyway, and the [[2026-Q3]] plan is honest about having room for two projects, not three. Revisit in March.

## What's already known

Basil wants sun and hates wind; the balcony has both, in that order. Parsley is nearly unkillable. Coriander bolts in summer heat regardless of what you do, so it's a spring and autumn crop or nothing.
