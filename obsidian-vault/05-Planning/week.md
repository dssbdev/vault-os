# Week 34 — Mon 17 to Sun 23 August

The first week of [[sprint|sprint W34-W35]]. Two live projects, and last sprint proved they don't both move in the same week — so this week belongs to [[recipe-box-app]] and [[home-backup-plan]] only gets its one purchase.

## Themes

**Build, don't plan.** The recipe project has been scoped, re-scoped and thought about for six weeks. Everything it needs is decided. The only useful thing left is typing.

**One irreversible step on the backups.** Ordering the drive is the step that makes the rest hard to postpone, which is exactly why it goes first.

**Protect the evenings that work.** Monday, Tuesday and Thursday are the reliable ones. Wednesday and Friday get nothing scheduled beyond the fallback meal.

## Fixed points

- Sat 22 — plan next week's meals, per [[meal-planning-basics]]
