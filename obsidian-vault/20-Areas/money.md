# Money

Spending on purpose instead of by default. Not investing advice, not a spreadsheet hobby - just knowing where it goes.

## How it runs

A [[zero-based-budget]] set once a month: every euro gets a job before the month starts. The weekly shop is the largest variable line and it became predictable the moment [[meal-planning-basics]] went in.

## Subscriptions

Reviewed in January and July - see [[2026-05-25-subscription-audit]] and the review rule in [[digital-life]]. The backup service from [[2026-08-02-backup-target-decision]] is the one subscription added on purpose this year.

## Standing problems

**Small recurring charges are invisible.** Each one is too small to notice and together they are a meal out every week. The twice-yearly review is the only defence that has worked.
