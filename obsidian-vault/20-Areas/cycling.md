# Cycling

Getting to work, getting outside on weekends, and keeping one bike working without it becoming a second hobby.

## How it runs

Commute Monday, Tuesday and Thursday — set up in [[bike-commute]]. One longer ride at the weekend when the weather allows and the kitchen plan doesn't need the morning. Ten-minute check every Sunday from [[bike-maintenance-basics]].

## Standing problems

**Wet days.** The rule is: if it's raining at departure, take the bus, no guilt. A rule decided once beats a negotiation at 07:40.

**Lights.** Batteries die on exactly the evening I forgot to charge them. A charging cable now lives next to the door.
