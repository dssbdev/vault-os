# Languages

English at work, Portuguese everywhere else, and the slow process of making the second one feel normal.

## How it runs

The [[learn-portuguese-a2]] project carries the structured part until November. Outside it: radio in the kitchen while cooking, shop conversations in Portuguese even when the other person switches to English, and vocabulary on the [[spaced-repetition]] schedule.

## What works

Listening while doing something else - cooking, cycling to the shop - adds up to more hours than any app ever did. *Devagar se vai ao longe*: slowly, you go far.
