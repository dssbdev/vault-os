# Home Kitchen

Everything about feeding myself well without it taking over the week. This is an area, not a project — there is no finish line, only a rhythm that either holds or slips.

## The rhythm

Plan on Saturday, shop once on Sunday, cook most evenings, eat leftovers at least twice. The single trip is the load-bearing part: every extra trip to the shop is where the plan quietly turns into buying whatever looks good and cooking none of it. The method behind the planning half is written up separately in [[meal-planning-basics]].

## What's always stocked

Rice, pasta, tinned tomatoes, tinned beans, onions, garlic, eggs, olive oil, frozen peas, a hard cheese. With those in the cupboard there is always a meal available, which matters far more than it sounds — the emergency meal is what stops a bad evening turning into a takeaway and a wasted shop.

## Standing problems

**Wednesday is where plans die.** By midweek the enthusiasm from Saturday is gone and the fresh vegetables are on their last day. The fix that has actually worked is putting the least demanding meal of the week on Wednesday on purpose, rather than hoping to be a better person that evening.

**Recipes are scattered across five places.** Screenshots, bookmarks, two notes apps, and the fridge door. Solving this is the whole point of [[recipe-box-app]].

**Portioning is guesswork.** Cooking for one reliably produces food for two and a half, which is fine when it becomes lunch and wasteful when it becomes a science experiment in the back of the fridge.
