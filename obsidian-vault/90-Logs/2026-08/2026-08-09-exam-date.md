# Booking the A2 exam for November

## Context

Five units into the [[portuguese-a2-course]], on schedule, and still no date. Without a date the course would drift into next year.

## Options considered

**The next session, in a few weeks.** Too soon - speaking is nowhere near ready.

**November.** Leaves a month after the course for a timed mock with the tutor.

**Next spring.** Comfortable, which is the problem.

## Decision

November. Confidence medium: the listening is behind, and the date is meant to fix that, not reward it.

## Revisit

After the mock exam on 2026-10-31. If speaking is still failing, move to spring without guilt.
