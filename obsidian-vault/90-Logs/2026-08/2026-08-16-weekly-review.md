# Weekly Review - 2026-08-16 (Mon 10 - Sun 16 August)

Second week of [[sprint-2026-W32-W33]], so this review also closed the sprint. It turned into the planning reset: [[2026-08-16-planning-reset]].

The recipe file format is decided and three recipes are written into it, so [[recipe-box-app]] has a real foundation. The list view was attempted on Friday and is half-built. The kitchen ran perfectly: five meals planned, five cooked, one shopping trip. Notes-wise, [[spaced-repetition]] went from bullet points to a real note.

## What slipped

The list view was on the plan Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday before being touched on Friday - see [[2026-08-13]] and [[2026-08-14]]. The drive for [[home-backup-plan]] was priced but not ordered. The phone photo backlog was carried all five days and moved nowhere.

## Patterns worth naming

**Hard tasks scheduled after easy ones don't happen.** Three days running, two small tasks consumed the evening and the one that mattered got what was left, which was nothing. The fix is position, not willpower: the difficult thing goes first or it goes nowhere.

**Carried tasks stop being visible.** The photo backlog has appeared on so many daily plans that I now read past it. A task that has been carried five times is not a task, it's a decision I haven't made - either block real time for it or drop it.

**The kitchen works and gets no attention.** [[home-kitchen]] has run without incident for weeks, precisely because the method in [[meal-planning-basics]] is simple and the fallback meal absorbs bad days. Worth copying that shape elsewhere: fewer moving parts, one deliberate escape hatch.
