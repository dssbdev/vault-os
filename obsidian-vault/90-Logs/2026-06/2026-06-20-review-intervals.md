# Do notes need flashcard intervals?

## Summary

Started writing up [[spaced-repetition]] and hit a question it doesn't answer: the classic intervals are designed for producing a fact from memory. Most of my notes aren't facts to recite - they're things I need to know *exist*, and where.

## Key decisions

- Vocabulary for [[portuguese-a2]] gets proper intervals. That's exactly the use case.
- Notes get one review the next day and one a week later. After that, links do the resurfacing.
- Left the bigger question open, marked ⚠️ in the note itself.

## Insights worth keeping

A method that fits one kind of knowledge perfectly gets applied to everything nearby. Worth checking the fit before copying the schedule.
