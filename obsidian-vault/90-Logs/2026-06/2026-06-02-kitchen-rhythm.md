# Setting up a kitchen rhythm that survives Wednesdays

## Summary

The meal-kit box was gone and the replacement had to be cheaper than the box without needing more willpower. Talked through what actually breaks: never the cooking, always the deciding — at 18:30, in the shop, hungry.

## Key decisions

- Plan Saturday, shop once Sunday. The single trip is the load-bearing part.
- The least demanding meal of the week goes on Wednesday on purpose.
- A fallback meal from cupboard staples exists every week.

## Insights worth keeping

Planning is a different person's job than cooking. Saturday-me plans; Wednesday-me just follows instructions. Written up properly in [[meal-planning-basics]].
