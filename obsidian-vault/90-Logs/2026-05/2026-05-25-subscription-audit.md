# First subscription audit

## Summary

Listed every recurring charge from three months of statements. Eleven of them. Four hadn't been opened in over a month; one was a free trial that converted in February and had been quietly charging since.

## Key decisions

- Cancel anything not opened in the last month. Resubscribing is easy; noticing is not.
- Review twice a year, January and July — rule now lives in [[digital-life]].
- Start a [[zero-based-budget]] so new subscriptions are a decision, not a default.

## Insights worth keeping

Subscriptions are designed to be forgotten, so the defence has to be scheduled, not remembered. Same lesson as [[habit-anchoring]], learned through the bank statement.
