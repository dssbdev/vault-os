# Would a home NAS replace the external drive?

Saw a small two-bay NAS recommended as "the backup". It's still one building, so under [[backup-rule-321]] it can only ever be the second copy — same job as the drive in [[home-backup-plan]], at four times the price and with a setup weekend attached. Probably no. Decide after the first copy exists, not before; deciding first is exactly the avoidance the plan warns about.
