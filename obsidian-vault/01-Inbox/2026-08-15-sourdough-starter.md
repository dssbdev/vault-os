# Sourdough starter - worth it or a trap?

A starter needs feeding every day or two, forever. That is a pet, not a recipe. Before it goes anywhere near [[home-kitchen]], check it against the rule from [[meal-planning-basics]]: does it make the week simpler or more fragile? First guess: more fragile. Park until the [[recipe-box-app]] ships.
